Common questions

How do I restore my subscription?
Open Settings → Everything and tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Does my history sync between devices?
Not yet. Your sessions and prints stay on the device where you made them. One subscription unlocks Everything on all your devices with the same Apple Account.

What's free?
