Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Sabi is a focus timer. It is built to work without knowing who you are.

What we collect

Sabi has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. To learn which parts of the app are used, it sends anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the EU. A signal says that something happened, for example that a 50-minute session was completed in a given scene and colour, or that the subscription screen was shown.

Signals never contain your name, email, location, contacts or anything you type. Each device is identified only by a random value that TelemetryDeck hashes before it leaves the device, so it cannot be traced back to you or matched with data from other apps. We use the signals only to improve Sabi; we don't sell them, share them or use them for advertising. TelemetryDeck's own privacy policy: telemetrydeck.com/privacy

What stays on your device

Your settings, your session history (date, length, scene, tag and the print each session leaves) and your colour choice are stored on your device only. If you use iCloud device backups, they are included in that backup by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether a subscription is active, and when you subscribe, an anonymous signal records which plan was chosen and its price, with nothing that identifies you.

Notifications

If you turn on the session-end alert, Sabi schedules a notification on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Sabi does not collect data from anyone, including children.
