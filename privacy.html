Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Sabi is a focus timer: pick a scene, set a timer and work. It doesn't need to know who you are, and we've built it so it doesn't.

Who we are

Sabi is provided by Alexandra Denisova, an independent developer based in the Czech Republic, who is responsible for your data as described in this policy. Contact: sabitimerapp@gmail.com

What we collect

Sabi doesn't require an account, show advertisements, or track your activity across other apps and websites.

We use TelemetryDeck for privacy-friendly analytics, to understand how Sabi is used and improve it. These analytics are events such as finishing onboarding, starting, finishing or ending a focus session early, starting a break, changing the colour palette, or opening the subscription screen.

An event can include:

  • For a session: its length in minutes, the scene and collection, the colour palette, the tag you chose (Study, Work, Read or Make), and whether you have Everything
  • For a break: its length in minutes
  • For the subscription screen: which part of the app it was opened from, and the scene that was selected
  • The app version and build, iOS version, device model and screen size, language and region settings, whether your device is in light or dark mode, accessibility settings such as bold text, reduced motion and text size, and whether the app came from the App Store or TestFlight
  • Your time zone, and the hour, day and week the event happened
  • Simple usage counts: how many times and on how many different days Sabi has been opened, how long a visit lasted, and the date it was first opened
  • A hashed identifier, derived from Apple's identifier for vendor, that tells one device from another without being linked to your name or Apple Account; a random session identifier that changes each time you come back to Sabi; and the time the event was created

We never send anything you type, your name, your email or your precise location. TelemetryDeck doesn't store IP addresses. Analytics are used to understand how Sabi works for people, never for advertising or cross-app tracking. You can learn more in TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

Where your data lives

On your device. Your settings, your session history (date, length, scene, tag and the print each session leaves) and your colour choice are stored on your device only. They aren't synced to iCloud and we don't run a server that stores them.

In your backups. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, Apple includes Sabi's data in that backup, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We can't access it.

On your Lock Screen. During a session, Sabi shows a Live Activity with the time left. It's created on your device and nothing from it is sent to us.

Purchases

Sabi Everything can be bought as a monthly or yearly subscription. Buying is optional; the free version keeps working.

Apple handles the payment, not us. The purchase happens through the App Store, so your card details, billing address and Apple Account are never seen by Sabi and never reach us. We can't charge you, refund you or cancel for you — that's all done through Apple, and you can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad.

What Sabi does is ask the App Store whether this Apple Account currently has Everything. We don't run a server for this and keep no record of your purchase.

What goes to analytics: that the subscription screen was shown and where it was opened from, and when a purchase is completed, which plan was bought and its price. No card details, no Apple Account, nothing that identifies you.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Sabi schedules one alert on your device for when a focus session ends. It shows the scene's name. No outside notification service is used and nothing is sent to us. You can turn notifications off in your device's Settings.

Your data and your choices

Deleting Sabi removes its data from that device. Copies in an existing device backup stay there until that backup is replaced or deleted.

Data retention

Your settings and session history stay on your device until you delete the app.

TelemetryDeck keeps anonymised analytics according to its own retention practices. These events aren't linked to an account, and we can't identify a single person's analytics history.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete personal data, and to object to certain processing. Because your data lives on your device, you can manage most of this directly by deleting the app. For anything else, email us at the address below. You may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority.

Children

Sabi doesn't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.
